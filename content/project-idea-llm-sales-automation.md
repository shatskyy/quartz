This note outlines a project idea focused on automating and intelligently personalizing cold outreach emails for [[Sales Development Representative]]s (SDRs). The goal is to leverage [[Large Language Models]] (LLMs) to generate and refine emails, making the process more efficient and effective for individuals like Julia, an SDR at an AI unicorn startup.

The project involves exploring how LLMs can assist in:
- Crafting initial outreach messages.
- Personalizing content based on prospect data.
- Automating follow-up sequences.
- Analyzing email performance and suggesting improvements.
