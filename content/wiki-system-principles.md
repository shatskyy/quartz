An effective [[Personal Knowledge Management]] (PKM) system, especially a wiki, should actively process and integrate new information rather than just indexing it. Key principles for such a system include:

1.  **Information Extraction and Integration**: The system should go beyond passive indexing to actively extract key information from new inputs and integrate it into existing knowledge. This involves updating [[entity pages]], revising topic summaries, and incorporating new data.
2.  **Contradiction Detection**: Mechanisms should be in place to flag or resolve new data that contradicts existing claims, strengthening or challenging the evolving synthesis of knowledge. This ensures the integrity and accuracy of the knowledge base.
3.  **Continuous Enrichment**: The wiki should become progressively richer with every new source added and every question asked. This implies a dynamic system that grows in depth and interconnectedness over time.
4.  **Automated Bookkeeping**: To be truly useful, a knowledge base needs automated support for summarizing, [[cross-referencing]], filing, and general maintenance.
