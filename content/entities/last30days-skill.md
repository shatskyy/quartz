# Last30Days Skill

The Last30Days Skill is an [[AI agent]] capability developed by `mvanhorn` designed to conduct comprehensive research across diverse online platforms and then [[synthesis|synthesize]] grounded summaries.

This skill automates the process of gathering recent and relevant information by querying:
- Reddit
- X (formerly Twitter)
- YouTube
- Hacker News (HN)
- Polymarket
- The broader web

By consolidating information from these varied sources, the Last30Days Skill aims to provide a more holistic and current overview of any given topic. Its focus on "grounded summaries" suggests an emphasis on factual accuracy derived from its retrieval process, contributing to more reliable [[knowledge creation]].
