The increasing consolidation of buyers around [[Vertical SaaS]] reflects a growing dissatisfaction with generic technology stacks. These generic solutions often lead to [[app sprawl]], fragmented data, and significant integration challenges, hindering operational efficiency.

Instead, the trend indicates a shift towards specialized, industry-specific platforms. This is driven by the fact that real work is increasingly executed within these vertical tools rather than general-purpose horizontal solutions. This allows for better data cohesion and a more tailored user experience, overcoming the limitations of fragmented data environments.
