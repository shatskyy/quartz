A [[Personal Knowledge Management]] (PKM) system built as a wiki can serve a multitude of use cases where knowledge accumulation and organization are critical. These include:

*   **Personal Tracking**: Managing personal goals, health data, psychological insights, and self-improvement efforts. This involves filing [[journal entries]], article summaries, and podcast notes to build a structured self-portrait.
*   **In-depth Research**: For topics requiring weeks or months of dedication, a wiki can incrementally build a comprehensive knowledge base from papers, articles, and reports, supporting an evolving thesis.
*   **Book Companion**: Creating a rich companion wiki while reading, with pages for characters, themes, plot threads, and their interconnections. This mimics the functionality of large fan wikis like Tolkien Gateway, but on a personal scale with LLM assistance for [[cross-referencing]] and maintenance.
*   **Competitive Analysis & Due Diligence**: Organizing information for strategic business functions.
*   **Trip Planning**: Consolidating details for travel.
*   **Course Notes**: Structuring academic information.
*   **Hobby Deep-Dives**: Any scenario involving accumulating knowledge that benefits from organization over scattered notes.
